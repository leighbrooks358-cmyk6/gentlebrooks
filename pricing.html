Pricing

Simple, transparent pricing.

No hidden fees. Pay per visit or save with a recurring plan. Final pricing is confirmed after your free consultation, based on care needs.

If your loved one is already enrolled in Medicare hospice: bathing and personal care from a hospice aide is typically covered 100% by the Medicare Hospice Benefit through your hospice provider — ask your hospice team what's included before paying privately. Gentle Brooks is generally used to supplement that care: additional visits beyond your hospice aide's schedule, more dedicated one-on-one time, or bedridden care for clients not currently enrolled in hospice.
