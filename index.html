Built For Bedridden & Hospice Care

Care that meets your loved one exactly where they are.

In-Bed Bathing & Comfort Care

A full bed bath for patients who cannot be moved or transferred — washing, hair care, and repositioning for comfort, done entirely in bed.

  • No transfers or lifting required
  • Hair washing & scalp care in bed
  • Gentle, unhurried pacing

Skin Integrity & Pressure-Sore Prevention

Extended bed rest carries real skin risk. Every visit includes a skin check and safe repositioning to help prevent pressure injuries.

  • Skin checks at every visit
  • Safe turning & repositioning technique
  • Early flagging of concerns to family & hospice nurse

Coordinated With Your Hospice Team

We work alongside your hospice nurse and existing care plan, not around it — sharing visit notes so everyone stays aligned.

  • Visit notes shared with family & hospice nurse on request
  • Consistent, familiar caregivers
  • A direct line to our care team
