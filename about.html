Our Story

Care for the moments that are hardest to ask for help with.

Gentle Brooks was founded on a simple belief: patients who can no longer get out of bed still deserve to be bathed with patience, skill, and dignity — not rushed through it.

Why We Started

When someone is bedridden or under hospice care, bathing becomes one of the hardest things for a family to manage alone — physically and emotionally. Gentle Brooks exists to take that weight off families, while making sure their loved one is treated with warmth, patience, and respect, entirely in the bed where they're most comfortable.

We built our service around three commitments: safety first, dignity always, and a pace that never feels rushed — and we work as a partner to your hospice team, not a replacement for it.

How We Vet Our Caregivers

  • Full background checks and reference verification
  • Hands-on training in in-bed bathing and safe repositioning technique
  • Dementia-care, hospice, and end-of-life sensitivity training
  • Ongoing supervision and check-ins
