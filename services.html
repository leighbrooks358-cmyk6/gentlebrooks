Our Services

Care built for bedridden and hospice patients.

Every visit happens in bed — no transfers, no lifting into a tub or shower. Built around comfort, dignity, and coordination with your hospice team.

In-Bed Bathing

A complete bath delivered entirely in bed, for patients who cannot be moved or transferred. Every visit — even our Basic tier — is a full, standard bed bath, not a stripped-down version.

  • Full-body bed bathing & perineal care
  • Hair washing & scalp care in bed
  • Higher tiers switch from basin bathing to our private inflatable tub & shower kit for a true soak-and-rinse bath
  • No lifting, transferring, or standing required
  • Gentle, unhurried pacing

Skin Integrity & Positioning

Extended time in bed raises real risk of skin breakdown. We check and reposition at every visit.

  • Skin & pressure-point checks every visit
  • Safe turning & repositioning technique
  • Early flagging of concerns to family and hospice nurse
  • Comfort-focused positioning, not just routine turning

Grooming & Oral Comfort Care

Every bed bath — including Basic — already includes standard shaving, nail trim, and oral hygiene care. Higher tiers add extended, spa-level versions of these plus more.

  • Shaving / basic grooming, included in every bath
  • Basic nail trim (fingers & toes), included in every bath
  • Oral hygiene care, included in every bath
  • Extended nail care and dry-mouth comfort care available on higher tiers
  • Fresh linens & dressing after bathing

More Ways We Help

Specialized care for specific situations.

Hospice Team Coordination

  • Visit notes shared with your hospice nurse on request
  • Scheduled to complement, not duplicate, hospice aide visits
  • One point of contact for your family

End-of-Life Comfort Care

  • Extra-gentle handling and pacing
  • Sensitivity to changing condition day to day
  • Available on short notice when possible

Dementia-Friendly Bed Bathing

  • Calm, familiar routines
  • Caregivers trained in dementia care
  • Reduced-anxiety techniques

Extended Bed-Rest Care

  • For bedridden clients not currently on hospice
  • Long-term illness, injury, or advanced age
  • Coordination with any home health nurses involved

Respite for Family Caregivers

  • A trusted break for family members
  • Scheduled or on-call visits
  • Clear notes after every visit

Family Communication

  • Notes after every visit
  • Direct line to your caregiver and our care team
  • Updates for family who live out of town
